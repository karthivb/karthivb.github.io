0DTE rules

Keep these visible

Risk first
1. One or two trades, max

Do not keep clicking to force a green day. Stop when your planned trade limit is reached.

2. Never average down

No adding to a losing contract. Define the exit before entry. A cheap option can still go to zero.

3. No FOMO or revenge

If you missed the move, let it go. After a loss, do not trade to win the money back.

4. Sideways = stand aside

Repeated VWAP crosses and overlapping candles can burn premium while price goes nowhere.

5. Respect the clock

0DTE options lose time value quickly near expiration. Avoid holding and hoping. Know the product's settlement and expiration details.

6. Daily target reached?

Manage/close positions according to your plan, verify you are flat, lock the session, and leave the screen.

You have another trading day. Protect your capital and your attention.
